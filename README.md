Saint Primitive Clothing

Mission: To provide stylish, affordable, and locally made clothing that helps young people express their identity with confidence.
<br>
Vision: To become one of South Africa’s leading independent streetwear brands by 2030, known for quality, creativity, and community impact.
 Target Audience: Youth aged 16-30 in urban areas. Students, young professionals, and T-Shirts who value unique designs and local brands.
<br>
Website Goals and Objective
Goals: 
  1. Sell products online directly to customers across South Africa
  2. Increase brand awareness and showcase new clothing drops
  3. Generate leads for custom orders and collaborations
<br>
KPIs to measure success: 
1. Achieve 500 online orders in the first 6 months
2. Increase website traffic by 60% within 3 months of launch
3. Maintain an online conversion rate of at least 3%
4. Grow email newsletter subscribers to 1000 in 6 months
<br>

Proposed Website Features and Functionality
Essential Pages: Home, About Us, Shop/Products, Price list, Contact.
Desired Functionality:
 1. E-commerce store with cart and secure online payments via PayFast
 2. Product filtering by size, color, and category
 4. Contact form and WhatsApp chat integration
 5. Instagram feed integration to show latest drops
 6. Responsive design for mobile-first shopping
